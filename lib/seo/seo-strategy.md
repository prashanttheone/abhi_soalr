## 7. SEO Content Clusters & Internal Linking Strategy

Create topic clusters around the following pillars:

1. **Solar Basics**: How solar works, types of panels, on-grid vs off-grid.
2. **Solar Pricing**: Cost of 1kW to 100kW systems, ROI, payback period.
3. **Solar Installation**: Space requirements, roof types, installation process.
4. **Solar Subsidy**: PM Surya Ghar, state-wise nodal policies.
5. **Solar Calculator**: Savings, system sizing, ROI tools.
6. **Solar Maintenance**: Cleaning, AMC, common repairs.

**Internal Linking Guidelines:**
- Always link Blog posts -> Relevant Product/Service pages.
- Link State-specific articles -> State Landing pages.
- Use context-rich anchor text (e.g., 'commercial solar ROI in Gujarat' instead of 'click here').

## 8. State Landing Page Architecture Template

For each state page (e.g., `/locations/states/maharashtra`):
1. **Hero Section**: 'Top Rated Solar EPC in [State] - Save up to 90% on Electricity Bills'
2. **Dynamic Data**: Current electricity tariff for residential/commercial in [State].
3. **Subsidy Info**: Detail the [State] specific nodal agency (e.g., GEDA, MEDA, RRECL) and PM Surya Ghar benefits.
4. **Service Clusters**: Link to Residential, Commercial, and Industrial solar specifically for [State].
5. **Major Cities Grid**: Links to city-level pages (e.g., Mumbai, Pune, Nagpur).
6. **Testimonials/Case Studies**: Show local [State] installations.
7. **FAQ Schema**: 'What is the solar subsidy in [State]?', 'How to apply for net metering in [State]?'.
